Chrome extension for the GST Portal

Your GST returns, saved from your own session.

Pack downloads your filed GSTR-3B and GSTR-1 returns and your auto-drafted GSTR-2B statements from the GST Portal session you already signed into. Pick the return and the period; Pack finds the document on the portal page and saves it to your computer.

What it never touches

Your sign-in stays yours.

  • password · OTP · CAPTCHAnever captured
  • cookies · session tokensnever captured
  • document uploadnone in the download flow
  • analytics · telemetrynone

How a run ends

Downloaded, or told why not.

  • Checked firstA document counts as downloaded only after Pack verifies it is complete and non-empty.
  • Only what the page hasA PDF or Excel workbook is saved only when the selected portal page provides it.
  • Interrupted runsPack keeps a local, redacted status so an interrupted run can be reviewed.
  • Cleaned upFiles staged in the browser are removed after a confirmed export, or when you discard them.
